Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-20

This policy covers the "vetted-pros-pins" application, a personal-use tool that publishes and reads Pin content for a single Pinterest account (the operator's own account) via the Pinterest API. It is not a consumer-facing product and is not used by, or on behalf of, any third party.

What data is accessed

  • The operator's own Pinterest account profile, boards, and Pins, via the scopes user_accounts:read, boards:read, pins:read, pins:write.
  • Basic performance metrics (impressions, clicks) for Pins the operator has posted.

What data is not collected

This application does not collect, store, or process data belonging to any Pinterest user other than the operator. It has no sign-up flow, no other-user login, and no advertising or tracking of site visitors.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the operator's own machine, not on any shared or third-party server, and are not shared with anyone.
